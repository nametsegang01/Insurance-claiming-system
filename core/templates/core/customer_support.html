Frequently Asked Questions

View All
How do I submit a claim?
To submit a claim, go to the Claims section and click "Submit Claim". Fill in the required details, upload supporting documents, and click submit. The AI will analyze your claim and provide a fraud risk score. Most claims are processed within 24-48 hours.
How long does claim processing take?
Claim processing typically takes 2-3 business days for low-risk claims. High-risk claims that require investigation may take 5-7 business days. You can track your claim status in real-time from the My Claims section.
How do I update my policy details?
You can update your policy details by going to My Policies, selecting the policy you want to update, and clicking the "Update" button. You can change your address, contact details, beneficiaries, and coverage amounts.
What documents are required for a claim?
For most claims, you'll need: A completed claim form, proof of identity, proof of ownership (where applicable), police report (for theft or accident), photographs of the incident, and any relevant supporting documents like medical reports or quotes.
How does AI fraud detection work?
Our AI analyzes multiple factors including claim amount, claim history, policy age, document completeness, behavioral patterns, and location consistency. It generates a fraud risk score (0-100%) which helps investigators identify suspicious claims quickly.
